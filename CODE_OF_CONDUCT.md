# Code of conduct

Streamfold follows the [Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/).

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Disagreement is expected;
harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and other
abusive conduct are not accepted.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when conduct is harmful.

Report concerns to [contact@assistant-ui.com](mailto:contact@assistant-ui.com).
Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Retaliation against a
reporter is itself a violation.
